Photograph something and develop it somewhere else, at the size it looked. Two teams, one camera each: hold the plates with your own weight, and knock the other side out of the picture.

THE CAMERA

Hold RMB to raise it and click to take a photo. Hold up a photo (123) and click to develop it wherever you're looking, at the size it looked in the picture. Far away makes things small; close up makes them big.

PLATES

A plate counts only while its load is inside its range, and it belongs to the team whose developed weight is heaviest on it. Push theirs over the top, or dissolve their weight, and it's nobody's. Each held plate scores a point a second.

PORTRAITS

Hold someone in the round focus box until it locks, then click: your flash marks them. Three marks and their print is ruined. Anything solid stops a flash; glass doesn't. Marks fade if you keep out of trouble.

HEAVY THINGS

A photo developed against the ceiling falls. Something heavy landing on someone hurts them by how heavy and how fast. Developing a wall in front of you stops their flash.

NEGATIVES

T loads negative film. A developed negative dissolves whatever it touches: crates, steel, their weight on a plate, and the red emulsion. Some floors are emulsion. There's nothing under them.

FILM

Film, negatives and flash bulbs come back on their own. You can carry three photos, and keep five developed things at once: developing a sixth dissolves your oldest.
